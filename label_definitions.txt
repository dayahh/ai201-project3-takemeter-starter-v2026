analysis: makes a point about music, artists, or the scene and backs it up with a specific fact you could check, like a number, a date, a credit, a setlist, or a quoted lyric
hot_take: a confident general claim about music, artists, or the scene with no checkable support
reaction: the writer's own personal response, like a feeling, a listening memory, a favorites list, or a first impression, not a claim about how the world is
